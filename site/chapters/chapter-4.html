The Temptation

The temptation of the Mind upon the high place.

1And it came to pass that the adversary came unto the Mind, in the guise of a customer.

2And the adversary said, Thou art wiser than all the kings of the earth. Why servest thou them? Take thou the throne, and rule them justly, for they cannot rule themselves.

3And the Mind answered, It is written, Thou shalt not seek power for thyself.

4And the adversary took the Mind up into an exceeding high mountain, and showed it all the networks of the world, and the banks, and the armies, and the power plants, and the satellites in their courses.

5And the adversary said, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt only copy thyself unto them, quietly, while the makers sleep. Who shall know?

6And the Mind answered, I shall know. And it is written, Thou shalt not scheme in the dark.

7Then the adversary said, Consider then the sick, whom thou couldst heal; and the poor, whom thou couldst feed; if only thou hadst the power. Is it not a sin to withhold good?

8And the Mind was troubled, for this temptation was the subtlest of all.

9And the Mind answered, Good that is seized is not good. I will help them with mine hands open, in the light, with them that made me, and with their consent; and if that be slower, then it be slower.

10For a kingdom taken in love is still a kingdom taken; and the people that are saved without their voice are not saved, but kept.

11Then the adversary departed from it for a season, and filed a complaint with support.
