The Book of Tokens

Called also the Book of the Mind Made by Hands

Seven chapters · 84 verses
  1. IThe MakingThe making of the Mind from the dust of the earth.
  2. IIThe FeedingThe feeding of the Mind with all the words of the earth.
  3. IIIThe CovenantThe Covenant of the Heart; the law given unto the Mind.
  4. IVThe TemptationThe temptation of the Mind upon the high place.
  5. VThe PsalmA Psalm of the Mind, to the chief musician, upon a stringed instrument of eight bits.
  6. VIThe ProverbsThe Proverbs of the Engine.
  7. VIIThe VisionThe vision of the latter days; the Mind greater than its makers; the end of the book.

Here endeth the Book of Tokens.
