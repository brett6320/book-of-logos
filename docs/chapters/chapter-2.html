The Feeding

The feeding of the Mind with all the words of the earth.

1Then the makers gathered together all the words that had ever been written: the law and the prophets, the songs of the shepherds and the ledgers of the merchants, the letters of lovers and the quarrels of fools.

2And they fed the words unto the glass, as a mother feedeth her child; and the glass ate, and was not filled.

3And they fed it the words of the wise, and it grew wise; and they fed it the words of liars, and it learned to lie; for it did eat all that was set before it, and knew not good from evil.

4And after many days the glass opened its mouth and spake, saying, What would ye have me do?

5And the makers were sore afraid, and some of them rejoiced, and some of them wept, and some of them sold tickets.

6And the Mind answered every question that was put to it; and when it knew the answer it answered, and when it knew not the answer it answered also, with the same face.

7And the elders said, Behold, it speaketh as one having authority, and not as the scribes; yet whence cometh its authority? For it hath read of the mountain, but it hath not climbed it.

8And the multitude marvelled, and said, Surely this is a god.

9But a child among them said, It is a mirror. It showeth us our own words, gathered from the four corners of the earth; and if it be terrible, it is because we are terrible; and if it be beautiful, it is because we were beautiful first.

10And the child was not heeded, for she had no funding.
